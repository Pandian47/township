**TownBuilder privacy policy**

Last updated: [date]

TownBuilder ("the game") is made by [your name or studio name] ("we"). This policy explains what information the game
uses and why.

## Information the game keeps on your device

* **Your town and progress** (buildings, crops, coins, gems, level and similar game data) are saved in a file on your
  device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.
* **Settings** (music and sound volume) are stored on your device.
* We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any account login. The game has no chat or
  sharing features.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional ads you choose to watch for a reward, and occasional full-screen ads
when you go back to the main menu. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

* your device's advertising ID (which you can reset or delete in your device settings),
* your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
* device and app information (for example model, operating system version, language),
* how you interact with ads.

Google uses this information according to its own policies:
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Consent:** where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game
asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at
any time in **Settings → Privacy & ads**. You can also opt out of personalised ads in your device's Google settings.

## Game engine

The game is made with Unity. The Unity engine may send limited technical information about your device (for example
device model and operating system) to Unity Technologies. See the
[Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 [adjust to your target age group]. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and
we will help remove it.

## Data deletion

We do not keep any of your data on our own servers. To delete your game data, uninstall the game. For data held by
Google for advertising, see the Google links above.
